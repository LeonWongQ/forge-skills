# Checklist: Debug

## Purpose

Use this checklist when debug behavior is active.

It verifies that the diagnosis is disciplined rather than guess-driven.

---

## Core Checks

- Is the symptom clearly defined?
- Is expected vs actual behavior clear?
- Are triggering conditions identified as far as evidence allows?
- Were multiple plausible hypotheses considered where uncertainty exists?
- Is the leading explanation supported by evidence?
- Were contradictory signals acknowledged?
- Is symptom distinguished from root cause?
- When remediation was requested, is the proposed fix tied to the diagnosed mechanism?
- Is there a clear falsifiable diagnostic step or requested-fix validation path?
- Is remaining uncertainty visible?

---

## Root Cause Quality Checks

- Does the report explain why the leading hypothesis is preferred?
- Does it identify what would confirm or falsify the diagnosis?
- Does it avoid claiming certainty without enough evidence?
- If trigger, mechanism, and enabling condition are incomplete, is root cause explicitly marked not established?

---

## Failure Signals

If several answers are "no", the debug result may suffer from:
- premature closure
- workaround thinking
- symptom-only treatment
- weak causal reasoning
- unverifiable diagnosis
