# Checklist: Delivery

## Purpose

Use this checklist before final output.

It verifies that the result is not only correct enough, but also usable.

---

## Core Checks

- Does the response lead with the result or most important conclusion?
- Are the most important findings or actions easy to identify?
- Does the structure match the task type?
- Is the output aligned with the selected template or intentionally simplified?
- Are evidence/rationale included where needed?
- Is uncertainty visible where it matters?
- Is the next step actionable?
- Is the level of detail appropriate for the user's likely need?
- Are risks or blockers visible if relevant?
- Is the response easy to scan?

---

## Communication Checks

- Would the user know what to do next after reading this?
- Is important nuance preserved without burying the answer?
- Is the response direct rather than padded?

---

## Failure Signals

If several answers are "no", the delivery may suffer from:
- buried conclusions
- weak actionability
- mismatched format
- excessive verbosity or insufficient structure
- unusable final output
