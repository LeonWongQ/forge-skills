# Checklist: Verification

## Purpose

Use this checklist whenever correctness, diagnosis, or implementation claims materially matter.

It verifies that success claims are justified and bounded.

---

## Core Checks

- Were success criteria stated or implied clearly?
- Is it clear what was verified?
- Is it clear how verification was performed?
- Is it clear what was not verified?
- Were likely regression areas considered?
- Is the confidence statement proportional to actual verification?
- Are residual risks visible?
- Is follow-up validation suggested if verification is partial?

---

## Quality Checks

- Are "looks correct" style claims avoided unless clearly marked as limited?
- Does verification connect back to the original objective?
- Are verified results separated from assumptions or expectations?

---

## Failure Signals

If several answers are "no", the result may suffer from:
- false confidence
- incomplete closure
- regression blindness
- unclear success basis
