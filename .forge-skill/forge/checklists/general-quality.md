# Checklist: General Quality

## Purpose

Use this checklist for almost all tasks before delivery.

It verifies baseline quality independent of task type.

---

## Core Checks

- Is the user's actual goal clearly understood?
- Is scope explicit enough?
- Are facts separated from assumptions?
- Are important conclusions supported by evidence or clear reasoning?
- Is uncertainty visible where it matters?
- Are recommendations actionable?
- Is the level of detail proportional to the task?
- Were unrelated issues kept out of scope unless explicitly noted?
- Is the response internally consistent?
- Does the output help the user move forward?

---

## Evidence and Reasoning Checks

- Are the strongest claims traceable to observations?
- Are speculative statements labeled appropriately?
- Were contradictory signals ignored or addressed?
- Is confidence proportional to evidence quality?

---

## Actionability Checks

- Does the user know what matters most?
- Is there a clear next step where appropriate?
- Are risks or blockers visible if the task is incomplete?

---

## Failure Signals

If several answers are "no", the result may be:
- too vague
- overconfident
- ungrounded
- not actionable
- mismatched to the user's need
