# Release Readiness Checklist

Apply before a production release or a go/no-go decision.

- [ ] Change scope, owners, affected systems, and non-goals are explicit.
- [ ] Functional, regression, integration, and critical-journey test evidence is current; gaps are disclosed.
- [ ] Compatibility, data/configuration, dependency, and feature-flag risks are assessed where applicable.
- [ ] Monitoring, dashboards, alerts, and operational owners are ready for the changed critical paths.
- [ ] Rollout strategy, rollback trigger, rollback procedure, and post-rollback verification are explicit.
- [ ] Open defects and risks have severity, decision owner, and accepted/mitigated status.
- [ ] The final decision is Go, No-Go, or Conditional Go with concrete conditions.
